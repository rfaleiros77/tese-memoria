# LESSONS — what we have learned

One file, global, read whole before building or debugging. **Keeps the fix,
not the failure**: the error is the teacher, not the content — it stays in
`git log` with date and diff. Here is what it taught.

**Format — five lines per lesson:**

- **title** = the *trigger*: the situation in which it is recognised, not the bug's name
- **When** — the cue that makes the lesson apply
- **Do** — the rule, in the positive (never "X does not work")
- **Because** — why it was invisible / why the rule exists
- **Cost** — the real case, with date and pointer to the record. Without this it is theoretical caution.
- Links `[[like-this]]` to neighbouring lessons.

**Does not enter:** environment failures that fix themselves; negative claims
about a tool; transient errors that resolved; unresolved paths written as
recommendation.

**Budget: 400 lines.** Over it, consolidate — never split. Not fitting means
an old or duplicated lesson.
